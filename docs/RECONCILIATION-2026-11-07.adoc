== The effort scale

One scale, mapped to the two axes that matter (intellectual difficulty and
time), because most items are one or the other:

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Label | Meaning

| effort:trivial | Near-trivial: a mechanical fix anyone can land in an hour. Several are good first issues.
| effort:small | Easy: cause and cure are clear; a day or less.
| effort:medium | Moderate: some design, testing care, or a few days of work.
| effort:large | Hard/complex technically, or a large measured sweep.
| effort:epic | Campaign scale: many repos, many weeks, needs owner cadence.
|===

Priorities (`priority:p0`…`p3`) are re-assessed against the state of the
tree today. P1 items are the ones that fester: they break CI foundations,
ship security exposure, or block a whole cluster behind them.
