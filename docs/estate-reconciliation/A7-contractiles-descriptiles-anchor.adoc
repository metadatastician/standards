= A7: Contractiles, Descriptiles and Anchor (clause modules, attestation, migration)
:version: 0.1.0-draft

Specification A7 of the Estate Reconciliation Plan. It defines how the contract between a repository's policy (the contractiles) and its reality (the descriptiles) is written in the chora deed. It also defines the anchor and the K9 envelope grammar, and the order in which the estate leaves A2ML behind.

== 1. Summary

* *Contractiles are policy, descriptiles are reality, and the deed attests both* [O: Q1 to Q4].
* There are six verbs, with two files per verb: `<verb>/<verb>.k9.ncl` (the declaration, in its `declaration` field) and `<verb>/<verb>.ncl` (the runner). There is no A2ML file, no per-verb manifest and no separate Verbfile [O: Q13].
* The descriptiles are clauses of the chora deed, never files [O: Q12, D312]. There is one descriptile clause per verb, plus META, ECOSYSTEM, STATE, AGENTIC, NEUROSYM and PLAYBOOK.
* The anchor is an `(anchor …)` clause of the chora deed. There is no `ANCHOR.deed` [O: Q11].
* Each deed clause gets its own ABNF clause module, following `clauses/egress.abnf`. `clauses/anchor.abnf` is the first (Part 3, section 6).
* The deed attests the contract with a `(contractiles …)` clause [O: Q14]. Its form is in section 8.
* K9 gets an envelope ABNF beside its Nickel contract [O: Q10]. Its form is in section 9.
* A7 adds no identity mechanism and no exception mechanism. Identity is A1's chora id. A known shortfall is an A2 `(deviation)`.
* A2ML leaves the estate one reader at a time and one repository at a time, never by sweep (section 11).

== 6. The `(anchor …)` clause [O: Q11]

Module: `1-formats/deed/spec/abnf/clauses/anchor.abnf` v1.0.0 (draft). Checked by `deed_lint.js --anchor`, and covered by the ABNF differential.

=== 6.1 Purpose

The anchor is the repo's semantic authority and golden path. It says what a downstream or satellite repo may extend but not redefine, and how to bring the repo back when it drifts. It is not identity (YL1).

=== 6.2 Shape

[source]
----
(anchor
  :schema "hyperpolymath.anchor/1"          ; exactly once
  :authority "upstream-canonical"           ; exactly once, non-empty
  :repo "owner/name"                        ; optional, informative only
  :purpose ("…")                            ; optional, non-empty list
  (metadata :version "1.0.0" :last-updated "2026-11-12")
  (identity :project "…" :kind "…" :one-sentence "…" :domain "…")   ; the SUBJECT, see J8
  (semantic-authority :policy "canonical" :owns ("…"))
  (implementation-policy :allowed ("…") :forbidden ("…"))           ; informative
  (golden-path :smoke-test-command ("just test") :success-criteria ("…"))
  (satellite-policy :must-pin-upstream #t :must-declare-authority #t
                    :must-have-anchor #t :must-have-golden-path #t)
  (semantic-authority-files :language-spec "SPEC.adoc")
  (ssg :engine "casket" :output-dir "public" :boj-trigger #t :cartridge "ssg-mcp")
  (parents (parent :uuid #uuid"00000000-0000-8000-8000-0000000000b1" :relation hub)))
----

Every member except `:schema` and `:authority` is optional, and every member appears at most once. The template's own clause at `a55669e` passes as written, mint tokens included [M].

=== 6.3 Rules the grammar cannot express (checked by the linter)

* At most one `(anchor …)`, at the top level of a `repo-deed` only.
* `:schema` and `:authority` exactly once. Any other member at most once.
* `:schema` carries the format major, so Q5's "wrong major refused" applies there: v1.0.0 accepts only `"hyperpolymath.anchor/1"`, and a minor revision of the profile adds no new spelling. `(metadata :version)` is the version of this anchor's content (the legacy `version` field, 6.4), not of the format: it has three segments and is not compared with the schema major. `:last-updated` is `YYYY-MM-DD`, or a mint token `{{NAME}}` that `repo-init` fills.
* `(parent :uuid)` is exactly once per parent, appears only once across `(parents …)`, and never equals the deed's own `:repo-uuid`.

=== 6.4 Translating a legacy anchor

[cols="2,3"]
|===
| Legacy `ANCHOR.a2ml` field | Destination

| `id` (reverse DNS, for example `org.hyperpolymath.standards`) | Dropped: the chora `:repo-uuid` is the id (YL1).
| `version` | `(anchor (metadata :version))`
| `clade` | Dropped from the anchor. If the repo wants a finding aid, it goes in the chora's optional `(clade …)` (A1 §11).
| `status` | Dropped from the anchor. The chora's `(status :phase)` holds it.
| `ssg.*` | `(anchor (ssg …))`, field for field (`output_dir` becomes `:output-dir`).
| `parents` (reverse-DNS names) | `(anchor (parents (parent :uuid #uuid"…")))`, using each parent's chora id from the registry. A parent with no id stays untranslated, and the translation is reported incomplete.
| `@profile(id=a2ml/anchor)` | Replaced by `:schema "hyperpolymath.anchor/1"`.
|===

This table is the anchor mapping spec that the ultraplan's P2.5 asked for. On ratification it moves to `1-formats/deed/mappings/anchor-to-repo-deed.adoc`.

== 7. The descriptile clauses [O: Q12, D312]

=== 7.1 One module per clause

Each clause below gets `1-formats/deed/spec/abnf/clauses/<clause>.abnf`, a profile in `deed_lint.js` (`--<clause>`), valid and invalid fixtures under `fixtures/<clause>/`, and an entry in the ABNF differential. That is the pattern `egress` and `anchor` set. Shared rules (dates, string lists, the affirmation members) go in `clauses/common.abnf` (J2). `anchor.abnf` moves its `str-list` rules there in a patch release.

Common members on every descriptile clause (DESCRIPTILE-SPEC §affirmation; J4):

----
affirm-member = ( %s":affirmed"    token-sep date-string )   ; YYYY-MM-DD
              / ( %s":affirmed-by" token-sep affirmer )
              / ( %s":expires"     token-sep date-string )
affirmer      = %s"owner" / %s"agent" / symbol                ; symbol: the check's name
----

=== 7.2 The six descriptive clauses (source: DESCRIPTILE-SPEC per-file schemas)

[cols="1,2,4,3"]
|===
| Descriptile | Clause | Members (R required, O optional) | Rules beyond the ABNF

| META | `(meta …)` | O `(adr :id :status :date :ref)` repeated, where `:ref` is the path of the ADR document. O `(development-practices …)`. O `:design-rationale` string. | An ADR kept as a document is referenced, never restated.
| ECOSYSTEM | `(ecosystem …)` | R `:project`, `:ecosystem`, `:position-type`, `:purpose`. O `:not` (list), `:pipeline-position`, `:chain`, `:coordination`. O `(related :name :relationship :notes [:uuid])` repeated. | `:project` equals the header `:canonical-name`. `:relationship` comes from a closed list: the template's 8 terms plus DESCRIPTILE-SPEC's 4 observed terms. `:uuid` is required in strict mode (J12).
| STATE | `(status …)` and `(maturity …)`, the existing clauses (J3) | `(status :phase …)`, with the template's phase values: A1 keeps `(status …)` as the template has it and defines no phase set of its own (A1 §5.4). `(maturity :level …)`. | The journal (last and next action) stays in `docs/status/ROADMAP.adoc`. A7 adds no `(state …)` clause.
| AGENTIC | `(agentic …)` | O `(patterns …)`, `:tools` (list), `:project-specific` string, and `(bot-directives …)` as the template has it. | No model pin. No language allow or ban list (YL3: that is the Mustfile's).
| NEUROSYM | `(neurosym …)` | R `(hypatia-config :scan-enabled :scan-depth :report-format)`. O `:rulesets` (list), `(neural-config …)`. | `(symbolic-rules)` is empty or absent. Rules live in the A5 registry, which this clause selects from.
| PLAYBOOK | `(playbook …)` | O `(deployment :steps (…))`, `(incident-response :steps (…))`, `(release-process :steps (…))`, `(maintenance-operations :targets (…))`. | Steps are ordered by position in the list. Version propagation is generated, never a step (DESCRIPTILE-SPEC's measured anti-pattern).
|===

=== 7.3 The six verb descriptiles [O: Q1, Q2, Q12, Q16]

Each verb's reality clause has the verb's name and sits at the top level of the repo deed: `(intend …)`, `(must …)`, `(trust …)`, `(adjust …)`, `(dust …)`, `(bust …)`. A clause of the same name nested elsewhere is not a descriptile, for example the template's `(policies (must …))`.

[source]
----
(adjust
  :affirmed "2026-11-12" :affirmed-by agent
  (fact :witnesses "adj-contrast" :value "4.8:1" :evidence measured
        :source "docs/a11y/contrast-report.adoc")
  (fact :witnesses "adj-keyboard-nav" :value #f :evidence tested))
----

* `:witnesses` names the `id` of a clause in the verb's `declaration` (`<verb>.k9.ncl`). That id is the join the runner grades (J5).
* `:evidence` comes from A2 D2's set. `:value` is any deed value.
* What each verb's facts are about follows the ruled meanings. `intend`: progress toward the north star. `must`: invariants. `trust`: security, privacy and safety. `adjust`: accessibility (ADJUST). `dust`: what has been stripped down, disabled or retired. `bust`: active failure pathways and the repairs applied, including partial ones (Q16).
* A fact never restates the clause it witnesses (YL3).

== 8. The `(contractiles …)` attestation [O: Q14]

=== 8.1 Shape

----
contractiles-clause = "(" %s"contractiles" 1*(token-sep verb-entry) [token-sep] ")"
verb-entry          = "(" %s"verb" 1*(token-sep verb-member) [token-sep] ")"
verb-member         = ( %s":name" token-sep verb-name )
                    / ( %s":component" token-sep string )   ; .machine_readable/contractiles/<verb>/<verb>.k9.ncl
                    / ( %s":runner" token-sep string )      ; .machine_readable/contractiles/<verb>/<verb>.ncl
verb-name           = %s"intend" / %s"must" / %s"trust" / %s"adjust" / %s"dust" / %s"bust"
----

Module: `clauses/contractiles.abnf`. The entry clause is `(verb :name must …)`, not `(must …)`, so that it can never be read as the verb's descriptile (7.3).

=== 8.2 Rules beyond the ABNF

* There is exactly one `(contractiles …)`, at the top level of a repo deed, with each of the six verbs exactly once [O: Q14; ultraplan P0.3].
* `:component` and `:runner` name the Q13 paths for that verb. Both exist in the repo; this is a repo check, not a lint check.
* `:contractiles_required` is derived from this clause. While it is still written (the template's `(policies (must :contractiles_required …))`), it must equal the derived set. After one release it is no longer written (J10).
* For every verb, the deed carries that verb's descriptile clause (7.3), so the runner always has both parties [O: Q12].

=== 8.3 What the deed does not say (J6)

The deed attests where each party is. The policy party is the component and runner named in `(contractiles …)`. The reality party is the verb's descriptile clause. The deed does not attest the verdict. The runner derives the verdict, writes a receipt and stops on a `must` or `trust` violation. A `must` or `trust` clause with no witness is a *blocking unresolved* verdict and stops the run too; no clause is left "deferred". A violated `intend`, `adjust`, `dust` or `bust` clause is reported at its own authority and does not stop the run [O: Q3; ultraplan P2.1]. The contract is then settled by renegotiating the contractile or by fixing the cause.

A shortfall the owner accepts for a time is an A2 deviation, never a field here:

[source]
----
(deviations
  (deviation :id "D-A7-1" :kind deferral :rule "contractile/adjust/adj-contrast"
             :reason "Theme rework lands next release." :authority "owner/standards#0000"
             :evidence measured :expires "2027-01-31" (scope)))
----

Scope, assumptions and boundaries stay on each contractile clause in `_base.ncl` [O: Q6]. Probe execution stays in the one signed `'Hunt` component [O: Q9], under the conditions of ultraplan P0.1, which A7 carries forward unchanged:

* A signature authorises `'Hunt` only when an L3 verifier, wired through `K9_SIG_VERIFIER` with a trusted-key policy, has verified it. `'Present_Unverified` never authorises it (`K9-CONTRACT-SPEC` §10.4).
* Execution needs all five preconditions: `signature`, `policy`, `sandbox`, `dry_run` and `capability_grant`. A missing one refuses the run and is named.
* While no verifier runs in CI, probes stay refused and the clauses they would witness are blocking unresolved verdicts, whatever the clause's verb: a refused probe is missing evidence, not a reported violation. They never run on an unverified signature.

== 9. The K9 envelope ABNF [O: Q10]

File: `1-formats/k9/spec/abnf/k9-envelope.abnf`. It grammars the envelope that `K9-CONTRACT-SPEC` §File envelope states in prose, and leaves the body to Nickel, which stays normative by reference.

----
k9-file        = magic-line header *skip-line dialect-line *line
                 ; K9-E005: the first significant line picks the dialect;
                 ; text that starts neither dialect matches no production
skip-line      = blank-line / *WSP comment-line           ; L0 skips indented comments too
blank-line     = *WSP LF
dialect-line   = ( component-start / coordination-start ) *line-char LF   ; no leading space, as in L0
magic-line     = %s"K9!" LF                                  ; K9-E001: exact, no BOM, no CR
header         = 1*4comment-line                             ; SPDX within the first five lines
comment-line   = "#" *line-char LF
spdx-line      = "#" SP %s"SPDX-License-Identifier:" SP 1*license-char LF
                 ; K9-E004 SEMANTIC: one comment-line among lines 2 to 5 is an spdx-line
line           = *line-char LF
line-char      = %x01-09 / %x0B-0C / %x0E-7F / UTF8-2 / UTF8-3 / UTF8-4
                 ; K9-E002: no NUL; K9-E003: no CR anywhere; UTF-8 well formed
license-char   = ALPHA / DIGIT / "." / "-" / "+" / "(" / ")" / SP
; K9-E006 SEMANTIC: no line longer than 998 octets, LF excluded.
; K9-E005 dialect, decided by the first significant line after the header:
component-start    = "{" / "[" / "(" / %s"let " / DQUOTE / identifier *SP "="
coordination-start = key ":" / "---"                         ; routed to coordination-k9-grammar_v1.2.abnf, which rejects "---" (D318)
identifier         = ( ALPHA / "_" ) *( ALPHA / DIGIT / "_" / "." / DQUOTE / "-" )   ; L0: [A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_."-]*
key                = ( ALPHA / "_" ) *( ALPHA / DIGIT / "_" / "." / "-" )            ; L0: [A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_.-]*
----

The `UTF8-n` rules are `deed.abnf`'s, unchanged. `identifier` and `key` are defined here, not imported: ABNF has no import, and the module must compile on its own for the differential. The two rules copy the character classes of `k9-validate.sh` L0 (lines 347 to 354 at the evidence commit), so that the grammar and L0 claim the same first lines. YK2 has one fixture for each dialect and one for unclaimed text, such as a first line `protocol` with no `:` or `=`, which matches no production and is K9-E005 in both. The step also amends the section of `K9-CONTRACT-SPEC` that explains why there is no `k9.abnf` (ultraplan P1.5), so that the section says the envelope has a grammar and the body does not.

The grammar must be `k9-validate.sh` L0's oracle, not a second description. Every envelope fixture under `1-formats/k9/tools/fixtures` gets the same verdict from the ABNF (through apg-js, as the deed differential does) and from L0 (test YK2).

== 11. Migration order: one repository at a time, never a sweep

Each step is one draft PR per repository, made only after the owner approves it. A step is done only when its acceptance test passes in that repository with the A2ML input absent.

. *M1, the gate.* Land Part 1a (#1488). Callers get it through the existing pin propagation. From then on, a repo can lose A2ML but cannot gain any.
. *M2, the generators.* `gv-clade-index` (1b), then `rsr-template-repo` (1c). The template also gets `#uuid` minting (J11), the `(contractiles …)` clause and the six verb descriptiles, so every new repo is born on the new forms [ultraplan P0.3, P3 "generator first"].
. *M3, the clause modules*, one PR each, in this order (the `contractiles` module lands with the generated `contractile` registry of J13): `anchor` (done in Part 3), `common`, `contractiles`, the six verb descriptiles, `ecosystem`, `status`/`maturity`, `meta`, `agentic`, `neurosym`, `playbook`. Each lands with its linter profile, fixtures and differential entry, then joins the caller-wide lint in `deed-conformance.yml` once ratified.
. *M4, standards converts itself.* In this order: add `(contractiles …)`; rewire the must gate and verb discovery (section 10, rows 1 and 2); translate `ANCHOR.a2ml` by 6.4; move the four all-caps `.contractile` files field by field into the verb descriptiles [O: Q12]; move the Debtfile into `(deviations)` (J9); make `0-AI-MANIFEST.deed` pointer-only [O: Q15]; then delete each A2ML file in the same PR that removes its last reader, which shrinks the ledger.
. *M5, the shared readers*, each with a new-input-first fallback: readme-derive, the debt ratchet, the RSR profile checker. Then hypatia (rules and `HYP-S005`) and the contractile CLI, each in its own repo.
. *M6, pilot.* Three repos of different shapes: one panoply-class, one deed-clean, one with live A2ML gates [R: ultraplan P3].
. *M7, the wave.* One PR per repo, worst first, from the census (ultraplan X1). Each renamed file is translated where a mapping spec exists and deleted where none does [O: Q7]. A repo is done when its lanes are green and no `.a2ml` file remains outside an archive. `CLAUDE.md` and other agent files are corrected in the same per-repo PR, never in a sweep.
