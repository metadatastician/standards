= Decision spec — per-directory `0.N-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml` ply manifests → deed era
Campaign: standards#837 · Frame: link:README.adoc[mappings/README] ·
Status: **PROPOSAL, not ruled.** Nothing here may be implemented until the
owner rules on §6. Written 2026-11-17 because a census of three repositories
found that this is the largest unmapped A2ML group: 127 of 360 files.

The per-directory manifests were folded into a deed once, by hand-driven
tooling, in the #837 pilot (`hyperpolymath/rsr-template-repo#176`, merged
2026-09-19). That pull request cites a "ratified mapping spec, Option A", but
no such document is in this directory: the mapping lives only in the pull
request's text and in the clause it produced. This spec writes that mapping
down, measures it against today's corpus, and asks for the rulings the pilot
could not have had, because the three-layer ruling of 2026-11-13 came after
it.

== 0. Naming collision (do not conflate)

Files named `*-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml` hold at least five different things. A
translator matches on the *content shape*, never on the file name (the rule
link:ai-manifest-decision.adoc[ai-manifest-decision] §0 already set).

Measured on 2026-11-17 over `metadatastician/standards` at `58362c2`,
`hyperpolymath/scaffoldia` at `8c833fb` and
`hyperpolymath/hybrid-automation-router` at `0176b7d`: 140 files of that name,
out of 360 `.a2ml` files in the three repositories.

[cols="3,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Content shape | Files | Governed by

| *META-shape*: `### [META]` and `### [AI_MANIFEST]` sections of `key: value` lines
| 127
| this spec (scaffoldia 77, hybrid-automation-router 50)

| *Stub*: an SPDX line at most, and one comment such as `# AI Manifest - Level 1: .github`
| 4
| this spec, §3.3

| *Prose*: Markdown headings and paragraphs under an A2ML name
| 6
| this spec, §3.4, for the 2 that describe a directory; §6 ask 5 for the other 4

| *Allocation manifest*: `[metadata]`, `[project]`, `[ai-allocation]`, `[policy]`, `[work-allocation]`
| 1
| link:ai-manifest-decision.adoc[ai-manifest-decision] (ruled option A, 2026-11-10)

| *Other records*: the cartridge directory manifest and the wiki-track pointer in this repository
| 2
| no spec; not this family (ai-manifest-decision §0)
|===

So the "0-AI-MANIFEST" family that was ruled on 2026-11-10 covers 1 of these
140 files, not all of them.

== 1. Source surface

`<repo>/<directory>/0.<N>-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml`, one per directory, and
`0-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml` in a directory other than the repository root. Every
repository minted from `rsr-template-repo` before 2026-09-19 carries the tree
(82 directories in the template at `9226f7e`).

The META-shape is not the TOML-like record dialect of the descriptile
families. It is YAML-like: `---` separators, `### [NAME]` section lines,
`key: value` scalars, `key: |` block scalars, two-space-indented
`key: "value"` maps and `- "item"` lists. That is a second reader for a
translator, with its own fail-closed list (§3.1).

[cols="2,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| Member (127 META-shape files) | Files | Note

| `[META] id`, `[AI_MANIFEST] description`
| 127
| Always present.

| `[META] level`, `[META] parent`
| 126
| `level` equals the ply in the file name in all 126. It equals the
  directory's depth in only 100: it is a *tier* (pillar, axis, unit), not a
  depth (pilot finding 2). `parent` resolves to an existing file in 124 and
  dangles in 2.

| `[AI_MANIFEST] canonical_locations`
| 26
| A map of role to relative path. 13 values in two files name a retired
  `.a2ml` file.

| `[AI_MANIFEST] invariants`
| 20
| A list of sentences, most containing MUST or "never".

| `version`, `context`, `purpose`, `[FILE_RELATIONSHIPS] files`,
  `[STAPELN_ECOSYSTEM] overview`, `[USAGE]`
| 1
| The container directory's manifest only.

| *nothing but* `id`, `level`, `parent` and a one-sentence `description`
| 97
| Such as `description: "Sub-registry for ai metadata."`. See §2 C.
|===

No `id` repeats inside either repository.

== 2. The options on the table

**A. A `(ply …)` clause on the chora deed** — the pilot's form, and what the
ultraplan of 2026-10-19 records ("Per-directory ply manifests fold into the
deed's `(ply …)` clause, as the template already did"). One clause, one
`(directory …)` per manifest, nested by containment (§3).
(+ A worked, owner-merged precedent with a byte-level golden pair: 82 source
files at `9226f7e` and the clause at `d49e4c8`. + Unblocks 127 files in two
waiting repositories now. − The owner ruled on 2026-11-13 that the chora deed
is identity and "is not organisation and not configuration". A directory map
is content organisation. Option A puts it in the identity layer, as `(meta …)`
and `(agentic …)` once sat there before J30 and J33 moved them out.)

**B. `CLADE.deed`, the content-organisation layer** — where the three-layer
ruling says a repository's own organisation belongs.
(+ The correct layer. − It cannot be done today. The CLADE form (A7 M3, the
`clade` module) has not landed, and `.claude/CLAUDE.md` says "Do not create it
yet". − Its ruled members are `(scheme …)`, `(category …)` and
`(placement :path :category)`: there is no member for a directory's
description, its canonical locations or its invariants, so the module would
have to grow before it could hold this content.)

**C. Do not translate: retire with a removal record** — on the ground that
the tree is derivable. 97 of 127 files state nothing but an id, a tier, a
parent link and one sentence that restates the directory's name. Under ruling
Q7 a file with no mapping is deleted, and the removal record
(`9-archive/A2ML-REMOVED.adoc`'s form: path, size, sha256, blob id) keeps it
findable in git history.
(+ No stale boilerplate enters the deed era. − 30 files do carry content that
is stated nowhere else: 26 canonical-location maps and 20 invariant lists.
Those would be lost from the working tree.)

**D. Split by kind (the aspect-oriented reading)** — treat the two kinds of
content differently, as A7 already does for `AGENTIC.a2ml`:
descriptive members (path, id, tier, description, canonical locations) go to
the organisation layer, A now and B later; an `invariants` entry is a
constraint, and a populated constraint is an edict, a `(rule …)` of
`Mustfile.deed` (A7 YL3), never a descriptile member.
(+ Each fact lands in the layer that can check it. − No translator can write
an edict from a sentence: a rule needs `(applies-when …)` and `(requires …)`.
So 20 files are refused until a person has written their rules.)

Recommendation: *A now, as a declared transitional form, with B as its
destination.* B is the most elegant and correct long-term home; the
recommendation departs from it only because B cannot be written or checked
yet, and 127 files wait on the answer. The transition is the one J30 and J33
set: when the `clade` module can hold the content, a chora `(ply …)` is warned
for one release and then refused in strict mode. For the invariants, see ask 2.

== 3. Field table (option A; a translator refuses anything else — P-2a)

The target is one top-level `(ply …)` clause of `<repo>_chora.deed`.

=== 3.1 META-shape

[cols="2,3,4",options="header"]
|===
| Source surface | Deed path | Transform / type

| the file's directory, relative to the repository root
| `(directory … :path "docs/governance")`
| STRING. Taken from where the file sits, never from its content. One
  `(directory …)` per source file.

| `[META] id`
| `(directory :id "governance-pillar" …)`
| verbatim STRING, first member. An id given twice in one repository is
  refused.

| `[META] level`
| `(directory … :ply 1)`
| INTEGER, verbatim. A `level` that differs from the ply in the file name is
  refused (0 of 126 today). With no `level`, the ply in the file name is used
  (1 file; the pilot did the same).

| `[META] parent`
| — (not written)
| Derivable, so dropped: nesting says it. Each `(directory …)` nests inside
  the `(directory …)` of its nearest ancestor directory that has a manifest,
  or sits at the top of `(ply …)`. The link is still *compared*: one that
  does not resolve to that ancestor's manifest, or to the root manifest for a
  top-level directory, is reported and never written (2 of 126 today; ask 3).

| `[META] version`
| `(directory … :version "1.0.0")`
| verbatim STRING.

| `[META] context`
| `(directory … :context ("…" "…"))`
| list of STRINGs, order kept.

| `[AI_MANIFEST] description`
| `(directory … :description "…")`
| STRING. A block scalar's lines joined by `\n`, with the indentation and
  the final line end removed.

| `[AI_MANIFEST] purpose`, `[STAPELN_ECOSYSTEM] overview`
| `(directory … :purpose "…" :overview "…")`
| STRINGs, as `description`.

| `[AI_MANIFEST] canonical_locations`
| `(directory … (canonical-locations :role "path" …))`
| one field per entry, the key verbatim as the keyword, the value a verbatim
  STRING, ordered by key. A comment inside the map is dropped (§4).

| `invariants` (in `[AI_MANIFEST]` or `[STAPELN_ECOSYSTEM]`)
| `(directory … :invariants ("…" "…"))`
| list of STRINGs, order kept: the text is the contract, as for the policy
  rules of ai-manifest-decision §3. Subject to ask 2.

| `[FILE_RELATIONSHIPS] files`
| `(directory … (file-relationships (file :name "…" :role "…" :description "…" :depends-on ("…")) …))`
| one `(file …)` per entry, in source order; `depends_on` → `:depends-on`,
  a list of STRINGs, `()` when empty.

| `[USAGE]` (`initialisation`, `development`, `production`)
| — (not written)
| Dropped: how-to text (§4).
|===

Member order inside a `(directory …)` is fixed: `:id`, `:ply`, `:path`,
`:version`, `:description`, `:purpose`, `:overview`, `:context`,
`(canonical-locations …)`, `:invariants`, `(file-relationships …)`, then the
nested directories ordered by `:path`. Sibling directories are ordered by
`:path`. A fixed order makes the output deterministic; deed order carries no
meaning.

=== 3.2 What the reader refuses

Any section other than the five named above; any key not in §3.1; a key in a
section other than the one §3.1 names for it; a scalar that is neither a
quoted string, a bare integer nor a `|` block; a tab; a list or map nested
deeper than §3.1 shows; a source that also parses as the record dialect. Each
refusal names the file and the line.

=== 3.3 Stub

A stub states no fact beyond its own name and place. It becomes
`(directory :ply N :path "…")`, with `N` from the file name, as the pilot
wrote it. Its comment is not a field: `verification/tests/0.2-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml`
in scaffoldia says "Level 1" under a ply-2 name, and the comment is the one
that is dropped. A stub with any line that is neither an SPDX line nor that
one comment is not a stub, and is refused.

=== 3.4 Prose manifests that describe a directory

Two files in the census (`.machine_readable/descriptiles/` and
`.machine_readable/descriptiles/anchors/`, each a `0-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml`). The
pilot mapped them by reading: the paragraph under `## Purpose` became
`:description`, the bullet list under `## Invariants` became `:invariants`,
`:ply` was `0`, and every other heading was dropped. That is a rule only for
exactly those headings: a prose file with a `## Purpose` paragraph, an
optional `## Invariants` bullet list, and otherwise only the headings
`## Canonical Locations`, `## Multiple Versions` and `## Protocol`, whose
bodies are dropped as instructions (§4). Any other heading is refused.
Subject to ask 5.

== 4. Drop doctrine

* `parent` links: derivable from nesting (§3.1).
* `[USAGE]`, and in prose manifests `## Canonical Locations`,
  `## Multiple Versions` and `## Protocol`: instructions, which live in
  documentation, never in a translated deed (frame README, drop doctrine).
  The `## Protocol` text describes locking A2ML files for concurrent writers;
  it has no subject once the files are gone.
* Comments, the `---` separators and the stub comment.
* Nothing else. A dropped line is counted and reported per file, never
  dropped silently.

== 5. Must-preserve hazards

. **Match on shape, not on name** (§0). The allocation manifest at a
  repository's root is a different family with a different target.
. **`level` is a tier, not a depth.** 26 of 126 differ from the directory's
  depth. A translator that "corrects" one to the depth destroys the pillar,
  axis and unit reading. `scripts/check-manifest-ply.sh` reads file names
  only and is unaffected.
. **Stale `.a2ml` pointers.** 13 canonical-location values name a retired
  descriptile (`state: "descriptiles/STATE.a2ml"`), and two invariants read
  "Metadata files MUST follow a2ml format". Carried verbatim, they put a
  recommendation of A2ML into a new deed, which `.claude/CLAUDE.md` names as
  a departure. kyX's own deed shows what a person did with them after its
  mint: `:anchor "kyx_chora.deed (anchor)"`. A translator cannot make that
  rewrite. Ask 4.
. **Invariants are constraints.** See option D and ask 2.
. **The two layers.** Whatever ask 1 rules, the clause never carries identity,
  and nothing in the chora deed's identity clauses is read from a manifest.
. **Generator first.** The template stopped shipping the tree on 2026-09-19,
  so no producer is left to convert. A repository that still has the tree was
  minted before that date.

== 6. Ruling asks

. *Home.* A (`(ply …)` on the chora deed, transitional, recommended), B
  (`CLADE.deed`: the most elegant long-term home, blocked until the `clade`
  module lands and grows), C (not translated; removal record) or D (split by
  kind).
. *Invariants.* (a) carried verbatim as `:invariants` and each one reported
  as a candidate edict (recommended: nothing is lost, and the report feeds
  the `Mustfile.deed` work); (b) refused while populated, until a
  `Mustfile.deed` rule holds each (the strict reading of YL3: most correct,
  and it blocks 20 files); (c) carried with no report (the pilot).
. *A `parent` link that does not resolve.* (a) nest by containment, report
  the link (recommended; the template's own tree has one, so (b) would refuse
  the pilot's golden source); (b) refuse the file.
. *Stale `.a2ml` pointers.* (a) a canonical-location entry whose value ends
  in `.a2ml` is dropped and reported, and an invariant or description that
  mentions A2ML is carried and reported (recommended: the entry states where
  a retired file lives, which is false in the deed era, and the test is
  mechanical); (b) everything carried verbatim and reported; (c) the file is
  refused until its source no longer names A2ML (deleting content from a
  legacy file is permitted; adding to one is not).
. *Prose shapes.* For the 2 that describe a directory: (a) §3.4's rule
  (recommended), or (b) refused, and retired under Q7 with a removal record.
  The other 4 are not directory manifests: the root front door of
  hybrid-automation-router (Q15 makes that file a pointer-only
  `0-AI-MANIFEST.deed`), and three files of this repository's
  `0-ai-gatekeeper` and `ensaid-config` specs, which describe the retired
  protocol and retire with it. This spec proposes to leave all four out.

== 7. Acceptance (once ruled; written for option A)

* *Golden parity.* From the 82 directory manifests of `rsr-template-repo` at
  `9226f7e`, the translator's `(ply …)` clause equals the clause of
  `rsr-template-repo_chora.deed` at `d49e4c8`, except where asks 2 to 4 rule
  a difference; each difference is listed, with its ask, in the test.
* *Count.* `(directory ` occurrences in a repository's `(ply …)` equal the
  number of its source manifests of the three shapes of §3.
* *Order-sensitive invariants.* For each directory, the `:invariants` list
  equals the source list, in order.
* *No rename.* No `0.N-AI-MANIFEST.deed` exists: the grammar dispatches no
  such name, and `deed_lint.js` refuses one.
* *Lint.* The chora deed that receives the clause passes
  `bun 1-formats/deed/tools/deed_lint.js`. `(ply …)` has no clause module, so
  the linter checks its grammar only; a module is A7 M3 work if ask 1 rules A
  for longer than the transition.
* *Refusals.* A file with a key outside §3.1, a `level` that contradicts its
  name, and a duplicate `id` are each refused, with the line.
